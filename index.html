How a level works

Every candy. Then home.

Every Halloween night Boo floats out of her pumpkin to snag the candy trick-or-treaters drop. Each level is a race: the stopwatch starts the moment you float off.

Grab every candy

The counter at the bottom of the screen shows how many are left. Miss one and the pumpkin stays shut.

Watch it light up

At zero, the plain pumpkin turns into a glowing jack-o'-lantern, and arrows point you the way home.

Float back inside

Boo zips into her pumpkin and the level is done. Three levels, one run, one score.

★★★Finish the level
★★★Beat the target time
★★★Beat the time with no bumps. Listen for the happy jingle.
Level score = 1000 + 10 for every second under par − 100 for every bump
Run score = every level you clear, added up. Your best ever is kept on the title screen.

Meet the neighborhood

Boo can't fight. Just dodge.

Every bump costs one of Boo's six health points. Run out and the night's over. Each trick-or-treater has a trick of their own.

Walks the plank

Pirate

Marches back and forth along a platform. Time your float over the top.

All wrapped up

Mummy

A bump wraps Boo in bandages and slows her down for a couple of seconds.

Big arms

Frankenstein

A bump throws Boo straight up into the air. Out of the way, at least.

Watch the wiggle

Witch

Waits, wiggles, then charges. The wiggle is your warning.

Surprisingly quick

Zombie

Also waits, wiggles and charges. Float up and let it run past.

Watch out for pits too: Boo sinks out of sight and bounces back a point lighter. The first screen of every level is always safe, and a med pac gives back two health.

Controls

Easy to float. Hard to master.

◀ ▶Float left and right
AFloat up. Tap for a little, hold for a lot. Up on the D-pad works too.
BBrake to a near-hover
▼Dive down faster
STARTPause. The clock stops too.

Ghost tips

  • Boo is always gently drifting down. Little taps of A keep her level.
  • She floats right through walls and the sides of platforms, and only lands on top of things.
  • Hold B and Down together and B wins: Boo brakes.
  • The game saves every time you clear a level. Pick CONTINUE on the title screen to pick up where you left off.
